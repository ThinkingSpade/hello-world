One recorded case, gate by gate. This is the same room the workbench runs — the auditors sit at their terminals, hand claims to each other, and walk to the boardroom when the council convenes. The only person who is not a model is the analyst: she comes out of the corner office at each gate, reads the bundle and signs, because that is the one thing the seats are not allowed to do.
